Comprehensive guide for Sandbox Testing & Live Merchant KYC Activation (Kenya)

Environment 1 Testing / Sandbox Mode (Ready Now)

You do not need business registration or KYC documents to test transactions in development. Everything is active right now using test credentials.

Sandbox Checklist:
  • IntaSend Account: Free account from intasend.com.
  • Test Keys: Copy Publishable & Secret API Keys from Settings → API Keys.
  • Mode: Keep INTASEND_IS_LIVE=false in your .env file.
  • Test M-Pesa Number: Use 254708374149 (OTP: 12345 if prompted).

Environment 2 Live / Production Requirements (KYC Verification)

To accept real money via M-Pesa STK Push and Credit/Debit Cards, IntaSend requires account verification based on your business structure:

Option A: Individual / Freelancer / Sole Proprietor

Recommended if you are an independent creator or developer without a registered company yet.

Requirement Details & Guidelines
National ID / Passport Clear color copy of Kenyan National ID (both sides) or valid Passport.
Personal KRA PIN Personal KRA PIN Certificate from KRA iTax portal.
Settlement Account Registered M-Pesa phone number or personal Kenyan bank account details for payouts.
Contact Info Valid email address, phone number, and residential address.

Option B: Registered Business / Limited Company

For registered companies, partnerships, or formal retail businesses.

Requirement Details & Guidelines
Registration Certificate Certificate of Incorporation or Business Name Registration (eCitizen / BRS).
Company KRA PIN KRA PIN registered in the name of the business entity.
CR12 Document Official CR12 document issued within the last 3–6 months listing directors.
Directors' ID & PIN National IDs and KRA PIN certificates of key directors.
Corporate Settlement Account Business Bank Account or Safaricom M-Pesa Paybill / Till Number.

tune Going Live: Configuration Steps

When your IntaSend account is verified and approved:

  1. Switch the toggle in your IntaSend dashboard from Sandbox to Live.
  2. Copy your Live Publishable Key and Live Secret Key.
  3. Update your production environment variables (e.g. in Vercel):
INTASEND_PUBLISHABLE_KEY=ISPubKey_live_xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx INTASEND_SECRET_KEY=ISSecretKey_live_xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx INTASEND_IS_LIVE=true
security Security & Data Protection

Bite Tech Ltd never stores customer card numbers or M-Pesa PINs. All transactions are tokenized and processed securely through IntaSend's PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant infrastructure.
